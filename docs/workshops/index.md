## Materials and facilitation

Download the {download}`shared fixture kit <assets/workshop-kit.zip>`. The offline scripts have executable assertions; live model and vendor-client exercises require your own environment. Work in pairs: one person executes, the other checks the evidence. Plan breaks between tracks rather than attempting every workshop in one sitting.
